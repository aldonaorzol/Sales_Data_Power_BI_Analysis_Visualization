The purpose of this analysis is to identify sales trends, determine the most popular product categories, evaluate the effectiveness of individual sales channels, and assess their impact on revenue generation. The dashboard enables users to quickly monitor sales performance and identify areas with the highest business potential.


## Analysis:

### Sales Channel Structure (Stacked Column Chart)

#### Purpose:
 Visualizes the distribution of orders across product categories and sales channels.

#### Insights:
- Identifies which sales channels generate the highest number of orders.
- Shows how customer purchasing behavior differs across categories.
- Enables comparison of category popularity within each sales channel.

#### Configuration:
X-axis: Category,
Y-axis: Count of Order_ID,
Legend: Sales_Channel.

### Percentage Share of Sales by Category (Pie Chart)
#### Purpose:
 Presents the contribution of individual product categories to total sales revenue.

#### Insights:
- Identifies the categories generating the highest revenue.
- Shows the overall sales structure.
- Helps determine which categories have the greatest impact on company performance.

#### Configuration:

Legend: Category
Values: Sum of Amount_ZL

### Percentage of Sales by Channel (Pie Chart)
#### Purpose:
 Illustrates how total sales revenue is distributed among available sales channels.

#### Insights:
- Identifies the most effective sales channel.
- Highlights channels contributing the largest share of revenue.
- Supports strategic decisions regarding channel investment and optimization.

#### Configuration:
Legend: Sales_Channel
Values: Sum of Amount_ZL

### Sales Over the Year (Stacked Area Chart)
#### Purpose:
 Displays monthly sales trends throughout the year.

#### Insights:
- Reveals seasonal sales patterns.
- Identifies periods of strong and weak performance.
- Helps evaluate business growth dynamics and sales fluctuations over time.

#### Configuration:
X-axis: Month
Y-axis: Sum of Amount_ZL

### AVG Basket Value (Card)
#### Purpose:
 Measures the average amount spent by customers per order.

#### Insights:
- Evaluates customer purchasing behavior.
- Indicates whether customers tend to place larger or smaller orders.
- Supports assessment of pricing and cross-selling strategies.

#### Metric:
Average Order Value

### The Best Month (Card)
#### Purpose:
 Highlights the month with the highest sales revenue.

#### Insights:
- Identifies the strongest sales period.
- Supports analysis of successful campaigns, seasonal effects, or promotions.

#### Metric:

Month with the highest Sum of Amount_ZL

### Sales Growth TOP vs AVG (Card)
#### Purpose:
 Measures the percentage difference between the best-performing month and the average sales level of all other months.

#### Insights:
- Quantifies how exceptional the strongest sales month was.
- Helps assess sales volatility and growth opportunities.
- Provides a quick indicator of peak performance.

#### Metric:

% Difference = Best Month Sales vs Average Sales of Remaining Months

### Category/Sales (Table)
#### Purpose:
 Provides detailed sales values for each category.

#### Insights:
- Enables precise comparison of category performance.
- Supports identification of high-value and underperforming categories.
- Complements visualizations with exact numerical values.

#### Columns:
Category and Sales
Allows users to view exact sales values for each category and easily compare category performance.

### Total Sales Value in 2025 (Stacked bar chart)
#### Purpose:
Compares total revenue generated by each product category.

#### Insights:
- Identifies top revenue-generating categories.
- Supports category prioritization and product strategy decisions.
- Allows easy ranking of category performance.

#### Configuration:
Y-axis: Category
X-axis: Sum of Amount_ZL

### Number of Orders in 2025 (Stacked bar chart)
#### Purpose:
 Compares the number of orders across categories.

#### Insights:
- Identifies categories with the highest customer demand.
- Determines whether high revenue is driven by sales volume or higher transaction values.
- Supports inventory and sales planning.

#### Configuration:
Y-axis: Category
X-axis: Count of Order_ID
